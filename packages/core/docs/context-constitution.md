## Floor (`CORE-*`) — never-do, non-overridable

| ID          | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CORE-01** | A re-render must **not** mutate an element's `status`. Only `modelling/` + `command/` may change it (`0`=new/transient, `1`=persisted/loaded, `2`=soft-dirty, `3`=soft-deleted). Re-render / reroute via `BaseElement.updateElement`, **never** through `reconcile`/`_builder`. | `_builder` flips any non-`1` status to `2`; a re-render path that runs it leaves `status="2"` residue that breaks the **byte-identical `toXML` round-trip** contract with `@d3-polytree/pfdn-moddle`. | `packages/core/src/draw/BaseElement.ts#BaseElement` (`_builder`, `status !== 1`), `packages/core/src/modelling/Links.ts#_rerouteLink` (+ its `status:0→2` residue comment), `packages/core/src/features/drag.ts` (`def.get('status') !== 1`) |

## Rules (`CORE-*`) — binding, easy-to-miss conventions

| ID          | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Why                                                                                                                                                                                    | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Example (canonical `path#anchor`)                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **CORE-02** | Drawer paint z-order is set by **DOM insert-before** in `_drawContainer`, independent of DI boot order: Zones append (behind), Links `insert('g', '.node-group')`, Nodes `insert('g', '.label-group')`.                                                                                                                        | A new drawer that inherits `BaseElement`'s plain `append` renders on top of everything regardless of when its module boots; the fix is the right `insert(...)`, not module reordering. | `packages/core/src/draw/Zones.ts#Zones`, `packages/core/src/draw/Links.ts#_drawContainer`, `packages/core/src/draw/Nodes.ts#_drawContainer`, `packages/core/src/draw/BaseElement.ts#_drawContainer`; scar `c659d32` (grid painted under background) | `packages/core/src/draw/Links.ts#_drawContainer`      |
| **CORE-03** | The deep `d3polytree.*` tokens (`.definitions.<node\|link\|zone\|label>`, `.settings.zoom/grid`) are didi property-path lookups off **one** `['value', host]` binding; the host must be bound as `value` (not `type`/`factory`), and `ensureSettings` must normalize the settings subtree **before boot** or injection throws. | Every drawer/settings-feature `$inject`s a deep sub-token; a missing sub-token or a non-`value` host throws at boot, not lazily.                                                       | `packages/core/src/model/model.ts#createModelModule`, `packages/core/src/model/model.ts#ensureSettings`, consumers `packages/core/src/draw/Nodes.ts#Nodes`, `packages/core/src/features/zoom.ts#Zoom`                                               | `packages/core/src/model/model.ts#ensureSettings`     |
| **CORE-04** | `ModellingLinks` is the **single** reroute writer, triggered **only** by `commandStack.changed`. Don't add `node.updated`/incident-node reroute listeners.                                                                                                                                                                     | The old bug (recorded in the source comment) double-routed and missed non-incident-node cases; one central reroute on stack change is the fix.                                         | `packages/core/src/modelling/Links.ts#ModellingLinks` (`init` → `on('commandStack.changed', …)` + comment), `packages/core/src/command/CommandStack.ts`                                                                                             | `packages/core/src/modelling/Links.ts#ModellingLinks` |
| **CORE-05** | Selection-delete is decoupled from the command stack via an **intent event**: `selection` emits `elements.delete`; `Modelling` converts it to one composite command. Don't call the stack directly from selection.                                                                                                             | Calling the stack directly fails in a **stackless `Viewer`** (the reason is stated in the source comment).                                                                             | `packages/core/src/features/selection.ts` (`emit('elements.delete', …)`), `packages/core/src/modelling/Modelling.ts#Modelling` (`_init`, + comment)                                                                                                 | `packages/core/src/modelling/Modelling.ts#Modelling`  |

## Norms (`CORE-*`) — defaults & asymmetry guidance

| ID           | Norm                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                             | Evidence                                                                                                                                                               | Example (canonical `path#anchor`)       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **CORE-N06** | A drawer with its **own** injected dep (Nodes/Links) must call `super(…, undefined, …)`, set the subclass field, then call `this._init(items)` a **second** time. Drawers without extra deps (Zones/Labels) pass `items` straight to `super`. | `BaseElement`'s ctor runs `_init`→`_createElement` before subclass fields exist; copying the Zones/Labels shape for a drawer-with-dep crashes on first paint (undefined field). | `packages/core/src/draw/Nodes.ts#Nodes`, `packages/core/src/draw/Links.ts#Links` vs `packages/core/src/draw/Zones.ts#Zones`, `packages/core/src/draw/Labels.ts#Labels` | `packages/core/src/draw/Nodes.ts#Nodes` |

## Gotchas & scars

- **Three feature modules are colocated in their own source file, not in `features/index.ts`:** `notificationsModule`, `autoLayoutModule`, `calculateCenterModule`. Grepping `features/index.ts` for "all modules" misses them. Evidence: `packages/core/src/features/notifications.ts#notificationsModule`, `packages/core/src/features/autoLayout.ts#autoLayoutModule`, `packages/core/src/utils/calculateCenter.ts#calculateCenterModule`.
- **`autoLayoutModule` omits `__init__` on purpose** (resolved on demand) and ships `layoutRunner: ['value', …]` as the documented last-wins override seam (swap a `WorkerLayoutRunner`). This is the intentional exception to `PLAT-02`. Evidence: `packages/core/src/features/autoLayout.ts#autoLayoutModule`.
